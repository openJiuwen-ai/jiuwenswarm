---
name: document-formatting
description: Read before formatting a document that mixes Chinese and Latin text, or before cleaning up a document converted from PDF or another editor.
---

**Mixed scripts.** Set the East Asian font separately from the Latin font when the document mixes scripts, so Chinese text does not fall back to a Latin font or the reverse. Keep indents in the unit the document uses: characters for Chinese body text (a first-line indent of two characters), points or centimetres otherwise.

**Direct formatting first.** When one paragraph looks different from its siblings, check for direct formatting overriding the style before you change the style, which would change every paragraph that uses it.

**Converted documents.** Documents from PDF or another editor often carry leftovers: manual line breaks inside paragraphs, tabs or spaces used for indentation, empty paragraphs used for spacing, text boxes holding ordinary text, every paragraph in Normal with direct formatting. When the user asks for a clean-up, replace these with real paragraphs, indents, spacing and styles, and keep the words unchanged. Report what you could not convert.
