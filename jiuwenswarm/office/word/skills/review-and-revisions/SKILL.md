---
name: review-and-revisions
description: Read before summarizing a review, or before rejecting tracked changes in a Word document, for the pitfalls Word's revision API has.
---

**Counts run low.** The number of tracked changes the API reports can be lower than what Word shows on complex revisions. Read the passage before you summarize what changed, and do not quote a count you did not check.

**Final text.** The final text includes pending insertions and leaves out pending deletions; say so when the distinction matters to the user's question.

**Rejecting a replacement.** Rejecting a tracked replacement can leave its inserted half behind or fail to restore the original exactly. Read the paragraph afterwards; when it is wrong, reject all tracked changes in that paragraph together and read it again.

**Summarizing a review.** Group the changes and comments by section, say who made them when the document shows it, and quote the changed words briefly rather than whole paragraphs.
