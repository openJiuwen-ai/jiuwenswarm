---
name: compose
description: Stitch finished shots into one film and mix audio by policy. Use when assembling the timeline, normalizing resolution, or ducking music under speech.
---

Stitch the finished shots in storyboard order and normalize the resolution. Do not reshoot a shot.

Apply the audio policy:

- Silent: no tracks.
- Speech: voiceover only.
- Music: music bed only.
- Both: duck the music under the speech.

If a shot is missing, stop and name the missing shot. Do not replace it with another shot.
