# Workday Health Plan

Collect or state assumptions for available days, session length, equipment, current
activity, sleep window, hydration constraints, injuries, chronic conditions, pregnancy,
and warning symptoms. Prefer a low-friction baseline that fits the user's workday.

- Build exercise from frequency, duration, intensity, warm-up, cooldown, substitutions,
  and stop signals. Progress one variable at a time; never require a precise load when
  the user's baseline is unknown.
- Spread hydration cues across the day instead of prescribing a universal fixed volume.
  Treat heat, exercise, illness, and clinician-directed fluid restrictions as reasons
  to individualize or seek professional advice.
- Use regular meal and sleep cues. Do not introduce intermittent fasting unless the
  user explicitly asks for it and the request is safe to discuss.
- Convert the plan into measurable daily and weekly actions plus a lightweight review.
- Do not write to long-term memory, create background jobs, or claim reminders were
  scheduled unless the runtime exposes that capability and the user explicitly asks.

For chest pain, fainting, severe breathlessness, acute injury, eating-disorder risk,
pregnancy concerns, medication conflicts, or clinician-managed conditions, stop the
generic plan and recommend qualified care. Hand the structured plan to the designated
check-in or page member as an internal result.
